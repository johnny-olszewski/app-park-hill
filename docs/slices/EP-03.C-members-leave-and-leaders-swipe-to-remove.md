# EP-03.C: Members leave and leaders swipe to remove

Members leave a group and leaders remove members with one confirmed tap, and nobody stays on a group they lost.

| # | Behavior acceptance criterion |
|---|---|
| B1 | A member taps Leave, confirms with the backend's wording, and lands on an updated Community tab |
| B2 | A leader swipes a member's row, confirms, and the member is gone; a leader cannot swipe another leader's row |
| B3 | Someone removed from a group while viewing it pushed is taken off it when they pull to refresh and sees "You're no longer in this group"; on Community home the tab just updates |
| B4 | A remove made stale by a change on the backend (the member became a leader) is refused with the backend's message, and the screen refreshes |
| B5 | All of the above on iOS and Android in Local. In Mock, B1 and B2 show the confirmation, the call and the dismiss or refresh; Mock responses are static (guide 6), so the updated state, B3 and B4 are shown in Local only |
